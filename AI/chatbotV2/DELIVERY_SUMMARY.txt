🎯 WHAT CAN IT DO?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ Read PDF Documents
   - Automatically loads all PDFs from knowledge_base/ folder
   - Extracts and indexes text content
   - Creates searchable embeddings

✅ Understand Questions
   - Accept voice or text input
   - Parse natural language
   - Find relevant document chunks

✅ Generate Smart Answers
   - Uses RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)
   - Provides context-aware responses
   - Works with local LLM or templates

✅ Speak Responses
   - Convert text to natural speech
   - Fully voice-based conversation
   - Human-like tone and speed

✅ Conversational Interaction
   - Multi-turn dialogue support
   - Maintains context
   - Natural language understanding

✅ Work Offline
   - No internet required
   - Local LLM support (Ollama)
   - Privacy-first design
