## 🏗️ System Architecture

```
User Input (Text/Voice)
        ↓
    [STT Module]
        ↓
    [Query Processing]
        ↓
[PDF Knowledge Base] ← Your PDFs
        ↓
[Vector Search]
        ↓
[Retrieved Context]
        ↓
[LLM AI Brain]
        ↓
    [Response Generated]
        ↓
    [TTS Module]
        ↓
User Output (Text/Voice)
```
